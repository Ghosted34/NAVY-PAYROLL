The window is open and forms are moving through the chain. This video covers the rest of your job: watching progress, keeping things moving, accepting confirmed forms, pushing them to payroll, and the record of what happened.

Progress Report is your formal picture. Filter by command and you get every ship with its personnel total broken into Not Filed, In Pipeline — meaning filled, reviewed or approved — and Completed. It is laid out as the Nigerian Navy E-Emolument Ship Completion Progress Report, with the production date on it, so it can go straight to a superior as evidence of where the exercise stands. Read it against the calendar. A ship with a high Not Filed count a week from the deadline needs a signal, not a surprise.

Authenticate Ships is there to keep things moving. Where a ship has a backlog of forms sitting at the same stage, you can clear them from here in one action instead of waiting for them to be worked through one at a time. Choose the ship, tick the forms or use Select All, and approve the selection. It is a throughput tool, so use it where the delay is the problem — a ship that has stalled, or a deadline that is close. Where the records themselves are in doubt, leave them with the ship. Whatever you clear here is recorded against you in the audit trail.

Accept Verified Forms is the last step of the approval chain. It lists every form the Central Pay Officers have confirmed, waiting on you. Filter by ship and by payroll class — officers, warrant officers, ratings A, B and C, and trainees. Open any form to see it in full together with its approval trail: who authenticated, who approved, who confirmed, and when. Tick the forms you are accepting, or Select All, and accept the batch. Accepted forms are then ready for the payroll update.

Payroll Update moves accepted emolument data into payroll. Choose the pay class — officers, warrant officers, ratings A, B or C, or trainees — and the system shows you a preview: every record that will be written, with service number, name and ship. Read the preview before you run it. This is the point at which the exercise becomes actual pay, and it is far easier to check now than to correct afterwards. Run the update, and it is recorded in the sync history below, so you always know which classes have been pushed and when.

View Tickets is your support queue. Personnel raise tickets from their own portal — wrong data on file, a form they cannot edit, an approval status that is not moving. Each ticket shows the service number, ship, subject and message, with a status of Open, Responded or Closed. Type your reply in Your Response; the officer sees it under My Tickets. Close it once resolved. Watch for the same subject repeating across a ship — that is usually a configuration problem rather than a user problem.

Finally, Audit Trail. Every change of consequence is recorded: which table, which record, what action, who performed it, when, and the before-and-after of what changed. Filter by table, by action, or by date range. When a query comes back on a payroll figure months later, this is the record that answers it — and it is the reason nothing in this system should be changed outside it.

Watch the progress report daily, clear bottlenecks before they become deadlines, read the preview before you sync, and let the audit trail do the remembering.
