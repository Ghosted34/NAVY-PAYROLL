# NARRATION ONLY

> Paste this block straight into your voice or avatar tool.

You hold the final approval in the emolument chain. Forms reach you only after a ship's Duty Officer has authenticated them and the Finance Officer has approved them. Your confirmation is what releases a form for payroll. This video shows you how to work at command level.

Sign in and select your command. Unlike ship officers, your access is scoped to a whole command — every ship and establishment under it. Your menu has four items: Dashboard, For Confirm, Bulk Confirm, and Confirmed.

Dashboard gives you the command at a glance: Total Staff, how many are For Confirmation, how many are Confirmed, and how many have been Rejected. Below that, the same figures broken down ship by ship. This is where you spot a ship that has stalled — one whose forms are not coming through — and chase it.

Open For Confirm. Every FO-approved form across your command is listed with the service number, name, rank and ship. Filter by ship, or by payroll class — officers, ratings or training. That lets you clear one ship or one class at a time rather than working through everything at once. Click a name to open the form read-only, with the full approval trail showing who authenticated it and who approved it, and when.

Satisfied, press Confirm. Not satisfied, press Reject with a remark — a reason is required, whatever the field label says. Rejection at your stage is the most expensive in the system. It clears the entire approval trail and returns the officer to a blank submission, which then has to pass the Duty Officer and the Finance Officer again before it comes back to you. Make your remark clear enough that it does not have to happen twice.

For volume, use Bulk Confirm. Filter to the ship and payroll class you want, tick individual rows or use Select All, and confirm the whole selection in one action. You can also confirm an entire payroll class at once. Use that only when the ship's returns are already reconciled — your confirmation is the last gate before payroll.

Confirmed lists everything you have cleared, filterable by ship and class. Use it to answer a query from a ship, or to check completion before a deadline. Confirmed forms then go to the Emolument Administrator, who accepts them and runs the payroll update.

Watch your dashboard for ships falling behind, confirm steadily rather than all at once at the deadline, and reject only with a remark someone can act on.
