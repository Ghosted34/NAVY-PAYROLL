# NARRATION ONLY

> Paste this block straight into your voice or avatar tool.

As a Duty Officer you are the first check on every emolument form submitted on your ship. Nothing reaches the Finance Officer until you have authenticated it. This video shows you how.

Sign in as normal. Because you hold a Duty Officer role, the system offers you your ship dashboard alongside your own personal form. Select your ship. Your access is tied to that ship. You see the personnel assigned to it and no others.

Your menu has three items: Dashboard, Pending Review, and Reviewed. Dashboard opens on Forms Awaiting Your Review — the count of submitted forms sitting with you right now.

Open Pending Review. Every form your personnel have submitted is listed with their service number, name, and the date it was submitted. Click a name to open the full form. You see it exactly as the officer filled it, but read-only. You cannot change their entries, and that is deliberate — the record must stay theirs.

Work through it properly. Are the passport photographs clear and recent? Does the rank, branch and ship match your ship records? Are the next of kin details complete, including their bank account? And most importantly — are the allowances claimed ones this officer is genuinely entitled to? You are the person on the ship who knows these personnel. Take the time here; it is far easier than unwinding an error after payroll has run.

If the form is correct, press Authenticate. Confirm, and it moves on to the Finance Officer. If something is wrong, press Reject. You must give a reason — the field is labelled optional, but a rejection will not go through without one. Say exactly what needs fixing; the officer sees that remark. "Wrong account number, digit six" helps them. "Incorrect" does not. A rejected form is reset completely — the approval trail is cleared and the officer starts again from a blank submission. It will come back to you, then go to the Finance Officer and the Central Pay Officer as if it were new. Rejecting is not a small correction, so use it where the form is genuinely wrong rather than untidy.

Reviewed shows everything you have already authenticated, with its current status. Use it to confirm a form has moved on, or to answer a query from one of your personnel. You can also add a remark when you authenticate, not only when you reject. If there is context the next officer should know, write it down.

Clear your queue regularly. Every form waiting with you is a member of your ship waiting on their pay.
