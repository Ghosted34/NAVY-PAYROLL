# NARRATION ONLY

> Paste this block straight into your voice or avatar tool.

You are the second approval on your ship. Forms reach you only after the Duty Officer has authenticated them. Your approval sends them up to Command. This video shows you how to work your queue, one at a time or in bulk.

Sign in and select your ship. Your access is scoped to that ship alone. Your menu has four items: Dashboard, For Approval, Bulk Approve, and Approved. Dashboard opens on Forms Awaiting FO Approval — what is sitting with you now.

Open For Approval. Each row shows the service number, the name, and a DO Reviewed marker confirming the Duty Officer has already passed it. Click a name to open the form. It is read-only — you are checking, not editing.

Your focus is different from the Duty Officer's. The Duty Officer confirms the person. You confirm the money. Check the salary account name, bank, branch and account number. Check every allowance claimed. Check the loan balances against what is already being deducted. If it is sound, press Approve and confirm. If it is not, press Reject and write a remark that tells the officer precisely what to fix. A reason is required, whatever the field label says. Be aware what rejection costs. It does not send the form back one step — it clears the whole approval trail and returns the officer to a blank submission, which then has to pass the Duty Officer again before it reaches you. Where a form is broadly right, it is often better to raise it with the ship than to reject it.

When your ship submits at the end of a cycle, you may have hundreds of forms at once. Bulk Approve is built for that. Every form waiting on you is listed. Tick the ones you are satisfied with, or use Select All, then approve the whole selection in one action. You can also approve by payroll class, so officers, ratings and trainees can be cleared as separate batches.

Bulk approval is fast, and speed is the risk. Only use it when you have already satisfied yourself about those records — for example a group you have checked against a ship return. Anything unusual, open it individually. Your approval is a signature.

Approved lists everything you have cleared, with its current status. Once Command has confirmed a form, the status here will show it. If a form you approved comes back rejected from Command, it returns to the officer to correct, and it will pass through the chain again.

Check the money carefully, approve promptly, and use bulk only where you are confident. Your ship's payroll depends on this queue moving.
